# Ideas to come back to: map and listing feedback

Date: 2026-11-13 · From the owner, after using the app on a laptop · Status: noted, not designed or built

These are ideas to consider later. Each one still needs the usual steps (talk it through, spec, plan, approval) before any building.

## Map

1. **Group overlapping price pins into a bubble.**
   - Where several listings sit close together, the price pins pile on top of each other and can't be read (for example near The Village by the Arboretum, and south of Stone Road).
   - Show one bubble with the number of listings in that small group instead.
   - When the student zooms in and there is enough room, the bubble breaks out into the individual price pins.
2. **More control over what the map shows.**
   - Example: switch individual bus routes on and off. With every route drawn, the map is busy and hard to read.
   - Other options are open for discussion.
3. **Links to transit information.**
   - A link to Guelph Transit, and to GO bus routes, somewhere on the site.

## Listing cards

4. **A visible "Click for more details →" on each card.**
   - Keep the whole card clickable; the label is there so students can see that it is.

## Listing details (laptop)

5. **Give the opened listing more room.**
   - When a listing is clicked, its details should take up more space.
   - Suggestion: the details go over the top of the filter sidebar as well as the list column.

## What a listing shows, and in what order

6. **Reorder the information from most to least important.**
   - Cost stays first.
   - After that, the order should follow what the student cares about, such as being close to public transit or close to a grocery store.
   - This needs a way to learn each student's housing needs.
7. **Listing titles in a fixed format: type of place + address.**
   - For example "Shared house, 164 Exhibition St" instead of "Quiet room on Exhibition St".
   - To be fleshed out when the landlord / posting side is worked on.

## Questions to settle when these are picked up

- Pin groups: should the bubble show only the count, or also the lowest price or how many fit the budget? What happens when a bubble is clicked (zoom in, or list the places)?
- Map controls: which routes are on by default (all, none, or only those serving the listings shown)? Should stops, campus and over-budget pins be switchable too?
- Student needs: asked once at the start, set in the profile, or picked from the filters? Grocery stores and similar places would need a new data source.
- Wider details panel: does the map stay visible beside it, and how does the student get back to the filters?
